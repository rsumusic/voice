ประวัติ

Biography

Parichat Euprasert, Head of Voice Studies at Rangsit University, specializes in Vocal Pedagogy and Music Therapy. With professional roots in creative advertising and art direction, she has nurtured many successful artists and frequently adjudicates major national singing competitions.

Education
  • Master of Music: Performance and Pedagogy, Rangsit University
  • Certificate: First Class Honor Presidential Medallion, Music Therapy, USA
  • Bachelor's Degree: Industrial Design, Chulalongkorn University
Professional Highlights
  • Vocal Coaching: Musical productions including Lion King, The Sound of Music, and Aladdin.
  • Featured Performances: Chorus member for David Foster and Andrea Bocelli Live in Bangkok.
  • Adjudication: KPN Awards, Yamaha YTMF, and ASEAN+3 Pop Singing Contest (Vietnam).
Academic Service

Regular Guest Speaker for THACCA (Music Boost Camp) and Alpha Skills Summit 2025 on "Enhancing Soft Skills with MUSIC."
