Biography

Areeya Rotjanadit, also known as "Lookpla," is widely recognized from her participation in The Voice Thailand 2018 and The Voice All Stars.

She has delivered numerous memorable vocal performances on the show and has had the opportunity to perform as a soloist with various orchestras, such as the RBSO (Royal Bangkok Symphony Orchestra) and Siam Sinfonietta.

Currently, she is an adjunct faculty member at the Conservatory of Music, Rangsit University, and a Vocal Trainer at BLKGEM (The Art of Academy), under GMM Grammy.
